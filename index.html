About

The Veganistas is a social action group based at the First Unitarian Universalist Church of Austin. We are dedicated to supporting and normalizing ethical veganism for individuals, the congregation, and the community. The Veganistas is a chapter of the Unitarian Universalist Animal Ministry

Ethical Veganism

Ethical veganism is a philosophy and way of living that seeks to exclude, as far as possible and practicable, all forms of animal exploitation and cruelty for food, clothing, or any other purpose. It extends beyond diet to recognize animals as sentient beings deserving of moral consideration, and it guides everyday choices toward compassion for all living creatures.
